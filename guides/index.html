Quick Onboarding & FAQ

Everything you need to know about setting up your avatar at Control & Chaos.

1. How do I join and start earning XP?

Teleport to the Main Arrival Terminal at Chaos Manor. Touch the terminal to register your avatar in the Experience cloud. Choose either the Dominant (DOM) or Submissive (SUB) path.

2. How do Profile Pick bonuses work?

Keep 'Control & Chaos' in your SL Profile Picks. Every 7 days you maintain the pick, you earn an escalating XP bonus and Gold Coin reward when touching the Weekly Terminal.

3. What happens in the Cage Break arena?

Submissives can be jailed in the cage by registered Dominants. Fellow submissives can hack the laser lock to free prisoners, awarding massive bonus XP to both players!

4. Where can I get in-world assistance?

Our Concierge and Mentors are available 24/7 at the Chaos Manor VIP Lounge. You can also join our in-world group for live announcements and tournament alerts.
