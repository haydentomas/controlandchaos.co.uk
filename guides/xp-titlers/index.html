1. Overview & What it Displays

The [C&C] Free XP Titler is an overhead floating text HUD provided free to all members of the Control & Chaos community. It queries your avatar's Experience Cloud record to render your active level, faction icon, rank title, and Prestige star rank above your head.

Displayed Information:
  • Line 1: Faction & Level — e.g. [ 👑 DOMINANT • LVL 48 ]
  • Line 2: Official Rank Title — e.g. « Sovereign Grace »
  • Line 3: Prestige Stars & Streak Bonus — e.g. ★ PRESTIGE I • 🔥 4-WK STREAK

2. Quick Start & Attachment

Step 1: Claim Free at Main Terminal

Touch the Main Welcome Terminal at Control & Chaos to receive your boxed starter pack.

Step 2: Attach to Avatar (Spine or Skull)

Right-click > Add the titler. Accept Experience permissions to link with your cloud profile.

Step 3: Customize Colors & Height

Type /66 menu to adjust the height offset above tall hair/hats or choose font color schemes.

3. Chat & Channel Commands

Command Channel Description
/66 menu 66 Opens titler settings dialog.
/66 hide 66 Temporarily hides the floating text (stealth mode).
/66 show 66 Restores floating text display.
/66 up / down 66 Adjusts floating vertical height offset.
/66 refresh 66 Forces immediate re-query of Experience XP cloud data.
