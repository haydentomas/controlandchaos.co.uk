ANSWERS & ASSISTANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about registering, ranks, spending coins, hardware compatibility, and sim protocols.

Is the Control & Chaos XP System free to join and play?

▼
Yes, 100% free! You do not need to spend any Linden Dollars to register, chat, sit on radar furniture, play Greedy dice, solve the Merge puzzle games, answer trivia, or climb through all 50 levels. Tributes and shopping simply offer bonus XP acceleration.

How do I choose between DOM and SUB factions?

▼
When you arrive at the sim, touch the Registration Terminal at the landing point. You will receive an Experience permission dialog and a menu to select either the Dominant (Empress/Master) or Submissive (Devoted/Bound) faction. You can request a faction change from sim staff if needed.

Do I need the Vow Collar or special mesh attachments to earn XP?

▼
No mesh collar is required to earn XP or level up. The Second Life Experience script operates entirely cloud-side. However, wearing certified hardware like the Vow Collar or PocketPig HUD unlocks automatic posture syncing, collar vanity badges, and RLV remote integration.

How do Gold Coins work compared to XP?

▼
XP is your permanent leveling score that determines your rank, title badge, and leaderboard standing (from Level 1 to Level 50). Gold Coins are the spendable currency you earn from quests, games, and coin hunts that can be exchanged at the terminal for items, vanity titles, and VIP lounge passes.

What are the daily Anti-AFK earning caps?

▼
To keep the sim vibrant and prevent unattended bot farming, passive earnings have healthy daily maximums (e.g., 250 XP/day for Social Sit radar, 150 Gold Coins/day for the Merge Game). Active gameplay like trivia, roleplay, and quests have generous separate caps that reset daily at midnight SLT.

Can I lose my XP, levels, or rank if I leave the sim?

▼
Never. All citizen statistics are securely stored in our cloud database via Second Life Experience KVP. Your rank, title, and Gold Coin balance will be waiting for you whenever you return to Control & Chaos or any affiliated estate parcel.

How do I become a Registered Dominant or get listed in the Directory?

▼
Verified Dominants who actively host sessions or maintain tribute jars on the sim can apply for a dedicated profile in our Service Providers Directory. Visit our Contact Concierge page or apply directly at the In-World Estate Office to set up your rate card and custom suite.

Are RLV restraints and FinDom interactions strictly consensual?

▼
Strictly and unconditionally consensual. All RLV devices respect viewer opt-in permissions and must feature immediate emergency release mechanisms. Our estate enforces a zero-tolerance policy against non-consensual griefing or harassment.

Official Sim Game Rules

Control & Chaos thrives on consensual discipline, high-energy gaming, and mutual respect. All residents, visitors, and service providers must adhere to these baseline rules:

1. Consensual FinDom & Boundaries

All financial domination, tipping, and service interactions are strictly consensual. Dominants may command, but harassment, out-of-character non-consensual demands, and real-life coercion are strictly prohibited and result in an immediate permanent ban.

2. RLV Safety & Restraint Protocols

When utilizing the Vow Collar suite or sim dungeon cages, safe-words and RLV release mechanisms must always be respected. Dominants must never abandon trapped submissives indefinitely without prior mutual agreement.

3. Fair Play & Anti-AFK Limits

XP and Gold Coin payouts enforce daily caps (e.g. 250 XP/day max for sitting, 150 Coins/day for Merge). Automated click bots or macro-spamming chat scripts will result in an automatic XP reset. Active human play always wins.

Verified Service Providers must honor their published rate cards. All Tribute Jars clearly disclose recipient splits. Never send L$ to unregistered third-party avatar alts without in-world confirmation.
