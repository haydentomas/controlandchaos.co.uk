📖 Click-by-Click Guides for Popular Registrars:
🟡 GoDaddy Step-by-Step
1. Go to My Products → Click DNS next to your domain.
2. Click Add New Record.
3. Set Type: CNAME | Name: @ | Value: controlandchaos.co.uk | TTL: 1 Hour.
4. Click Save.
🟠 Namecheap Step-by-Step
1. Go to Domain List → Click Manage next to your domain.
2. Click the Advanced DNS tab.
3. Click Add New Record → Select CNAME Record.
4. Host: @ | Target: controlandchaos.co.uk | TTL: Automatic.
5. Click the green checkmark (Save).
🔵 Cloudflare Step-by-Step
1. Select your domain → Click DNS → Records.
2. Click Add record.
3. Type: CNAME | Name: @ | Target: controlandchaos.co.uk | Proxy Status: DNS Only (Grey Cloud).
4. Click Save.
⏳ Note: DNS changes typically take between 5 to 30 minutes to propagate across the internet. Once live, anyone visiting your domain will see your verified rate card immediately.
